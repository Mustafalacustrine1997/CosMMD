## 🔄 How It Works (Behind the Scenes)

For the curious ones, here's what happens when you use CosMMD:

1. **Photo Analysis** - The AI examines your photo, identifying the person, their pose, and their features
2. **3D Model Generation** - Nano Banana Pro creates a detailed 3D mesh of the person
3. **Texture Mapping** - The photo's colors and details are applied to the 3D model
4. **Rigging** - A digital skeleton is added, with special attention to facial features and fingers
5. **Dance Integration** - MMD dance data is applied to the rigged model
6. **Rendering** - The final animation is rendered into a video file

All of this happens automatically. You just provide the photo and pick the dance.
